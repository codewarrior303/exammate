ExamMate offline

Your saved routine is available on this device. You can also parse an Excel or CSV routine without an internet connection.

Loading your saved routine…

Routine data stays in this browser's local storage. Excel files are parsed on this device and are not uploaded. PDF and PNG downloads work offline; Print PDF needs an internet connection. Drive import and Google Calendar sync also need internet.

Privacy Policy

Offline copy. ExamMate processes spreadsheet uploads on your device as data; it does not run embedded macros or scripts. Shared Google Drive/Sheets links are fetched temporarily by the server only when online, then returned to your browser for parsing. Private links are not opened using your Google account, and ExamMate never asks for your Google password.

Routine and account data

Your generated routine is stored in this browser for up to 15 days. Google sign-in is optional. If you connect Calendar, ExamMate uses your basic account identity and the Calendar permission you approve to create or update ExamMate exam events. Google handles event reminders.

Calendar sync sends selected course details, faculty if present, exam date/time, reminder settings, and event description to Google Calendar. ExamMate does not read your existing events or delete calendar events. Events already created remain in your calendar after disconnecting.

Storage and providers

Account and encrypted OAuth records are held server-side while your account is connected. ExamMate uses Google for sign-in/calendar, MongoDB for account records, and Vercel for hosting, analytics, and performance measurements. It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI/ML models.

Your choices

Google Calendar sync is optional. You can clear a locally saved routine, disconnect Google, or revoke Google access in your Google Account. This offline policy summary may not include later changes; reconnect to review the current online policy.

Questions or deletion requests: exammate.support@gmail.com

Terms and Conditions

ExamMate is a free student productivity tool for personal academic planning and convenience.

Academic responsibility

ExamMate parses and organizes routine data you provide. It does not replace official university notices or regulations. Verify your timetable against official Southeast University publications.

Files and Google Drive links

You are responsible for having legitimate access to any spreadsheet or Drive link you provide. Device uploads are parsed locally; shared Drive links require an internet connection and are temporarily fetched to return them to your browser.

Local data and Calendar

Your routine is stored in this browser for up to 15 days and can be removed with Clear My Data. Calendar sync is optional and creates exam events only after you connect Google and authorize the feature.

Service availability

ExamMate is provided as-is. Offline use is limited to the cached workspace, previously saved routines, local file parsing, and local PDF/PNG exports.
